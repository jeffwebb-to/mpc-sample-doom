DOOM for MPC Sample
===================

What this is
------------
DOOM, the 1993 game, running on your MPC Sample's screen and pads from an SD card. It uses
the open-source doomgeneric engine. Nothing is installed on the device. The card starts DOOM
in RAM, and the sampler comes back when you quit. Take the card out and power cycle and the
MPC is exactly as it was. It comes with Freedoom, a free set of game data. If you own DOOM
you can play the real levels with your own WAD file.

Read this first
---------------
This is an unofficial hack. It is built so that it cannot change the device's firmware or
internal storage, but you use it at your own risk. The real risk is losing data, and you
can remove that risk before you start:

1. SAVE THE PROJECT THAT IS OPEN ON THE MPC SAMPLE. Starting DOOM closes the sampler app
   the same way powering off does. Do not rely on the app's temporary project to keep
   unsaved edits.
2. USE A FRESH SD CARD, OR BACK UP THE CARD FIRST. Copy the whole card to a computer
   before you put the DOOM files on it. A spare card with only the DOOM files on it is
   the simplest and safest option.
3. BACK UP THE PROJECTS AND SAMPLES ON THE DEVICE. Export them or copy them over USB. Do
   this before trying any modification.
4. If your card already has an "autorun" folder from something else, keep a copy of it.
   The DOOM "autorun" folder replaces it.

Setup
-----
Do the backups above first.

1. Unzip. Copy the "autorun" folder and the "doom" folder to the root of your SD card, next
   to your own folders. The card should then contain autorun/autorun.json, autorun/doom.sh,
   autorun/autorun.check, autorun/doom and doom/freedoom1.wad. Nothing else on the card is
   read or written.
2. Optional: put your own doom.wad or doom2.wad in the card's "doom" folder. It will be used
   instead of Freedoom. DOOM.WAD and DOOM2.WAD in capitals work too.
3. Save your project on the MPC Sample.
4. Put the card in. DOOM starts a few seconds after the sampler has booted, every time the
   device is powered on with the card in. Take the card out if you want a normal boot.

Controls
--------
  PAD 1   strafe left        PAD 5   fire
  PAD 2   walk back          PAD 6   use (doors, switches)
  PAD 3   walk forward       PAD 7   run (hold)
  PAD 4   strafe right       PAD 8   next weapon
  PAD 1 is bottom left. Pads 9 to 16 show your health as a bar.

  DATA knob      turn left or right (moves the cursor in menus)
  DATA push      Enter
  SHIFT          Esc (menu, back)
  PLUS / MINUS   menu down / up
  PLAY / STOP    Yes / No when the game asks
  TAP TEMPO      automap on and off

  Hold SHIFT and press STOP to quit DOOM and go back to the sampler. You can also pick
  Quit Game in the menu and press PLAY for Yes.

If something goes wrong
-----------------------
- Nothing happens: check that "autorun" and "doom" are at the root of the card, not inside
  another folder, and that all four files are in "autorun". Do not edit the files in
  "autorun"; the device refuses to run them if they are changed.
- Black screen and no DOOM: wait ten seconds. If the sampler does not come back, take the
  card out and power cycle. The device boots normally without the card.
- DOOM freezes: take the card out and power cycle. The sampler was already shut down
  cleanly before DOOM started, so this is no worse than a normal power-off.
- The sampler does not come back after quitting: power cycle with the card out.
- Loud: DOOM plays at whatever volume the sampler left. Turn it down before you start,
  especially with headphones.

Notes
-----
- Sound effects play through the speaker, or through headphones if they are plugged in.
  Sound has been tested in the emulator but not yet confirmed on a real device. There is
  no music.
- Saved games and settings live in RAM and are gone at power-off.
- Tested on firmware 1.3.0.12 only.
